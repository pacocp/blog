Sometimes I think of language as a representation of the worlds we live in. I describe what I see, with the words that we have assigned to them. When you are a kid, people point to things, and you understand the link between the word and the object.

There is something that does not match there, and are feelings. How can we assign a name to something that we are feeling? People tell us: I am mad, sad, happy, and we need to link their sensation to ours. But, is it the same? What if the same feeling could describe multiple ways of approaching it. That's sometimes how we describe the language of love. The base feeling should be the same, but it can be interpreted or manifested in a different way depending on the person.

Puting words to what we feel is crucial for relationships of any kind. I am trying to put more emphasis on describing how I feel and for what I feel that way. I think it would help me and others.
